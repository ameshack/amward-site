The challenge
Persuade residents to choose reusable bags, cups and utensils over disposable ones.
My role
Commissioned and directed the campaign, leading the agency, creative vendors and contractors.
Explore the project: Integrated brand campaign and audience engagement

Strategy and my role

Commissioned and directed a fashion-inspired campaign encouraging residents to choose reusable bags, cups and utensils over disposable alternatives. Led the agency, creative vendors and contractors across integrated advertising and community activations.

Execution

Combined advertising, professional-photo pop-ups, reusable giveaways, a pledge website and geotargeted digital promotion that drove attendance at neighborhood activations. Culturally relevant creative reached Spanish- and Chinese-speaking, lower-income and older residents.

Results

  • +51% intent to reuse
  • Video engagement at 7× benchmark
  • Thousands of pledges and 4,000+ participant photos shared on social media
  • 2022 ADDY Award, Public Service Campaign

Campaign-level results reported by the agency partner. The 51% measures intent to reuse, not verified behavior.

City & County of San Francisco · SF Climate Action

Brand architecture and governance

A unified climate brand system, and the citywide initiative built on it

The challenge
Bring the city’s environmental services and climate goals together under one coherent brand.
My role
Expanded the city’s climate identity into a brand system, set its governance and created Climate Action Month to extend it.
Explore the project: Brand architecture and governance

Strategy and my role

Expanded the city’s “0/80/100/Roots” climate identity into a brand system connecting program identities, digital experiences, community outreach and executive communications. Established naming and messaging governance for new products and citywide initiatives.

Extending the brand

Created Climate Action Month, an annual citywide initiative that carried the brand to residents through an integrated partner strategy and a multilingual event-discovery experience.

Citywide context

  • San Francisco was named to the CDP (formerly Carbon Disclosure Project) Cities A List for the fourth consecutive year in 2022, recognizing global leadership in environmental transparency and climate action
  • The city reduced its carbon emissions while its population and economy grew, supported by the San Francisco Climate Action Plan and community engagement

City-level results that the brand system and public engagement supported; they reflect the work of many departments and partners.
