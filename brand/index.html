Delta Air Lines · Sustainability brand campaign

National brand campaign

#1tested Delta ad for driving consumer brand difference and affinity

Liftstatistically significant lift in association with Delta’s net-zero-by-2050 commitment

The challenge
Stand out with consumers nationally and deepen their affinity for the Delta brand.
My role
Led strategy and execution for a national omnichannel campaign, from consumer research and creative briefs through agency direction, paid media and measurement.
Strategic approach
Turned consumer research into creative briefs that spotlighted tangible changes to Delta’s operations, rather than distant climate goals.
Explore the project: National brand campaign

Strategic insight

Consumer research reinforced the value of concrete, informative sustainability claims in building brand differentiation and affinity. By spotlighting tangible changes to Delta’s operations, the campaign made progress toward its long-term climate commitments more relatable, credible and memorable.

Research and execution

Led multiphase qualitative and quantitative consumer research and competitive analysis, translated the findings into creative briefs and agency direction, and oversaw paid-media investment, measurement and reporting.

Results

  • The #1 tested Delta ad for driving consumer brand difference and affinity, in the emotive-testing phase of Delta consumer research
  • Statistically significant lift in association with the message “We are committed to net zero carbon emissions by 2050,” per the media agency’s mid-campaign report

Message association measures what viewers linked to Delta, not progress on emissions.

Ongoing content strategy

Separately, developed a research-backed evergreen content strategy built on four principles: spotlight current progress over distant commitments, feature the employees and partners behind it, connect progress to customer benefits, and share setbacks as openly as gains. It guided ongoing content planning, not the campaign ad.

City & County of San Francisco · REUSE SF

Integrated brand campaign and audience engagement

+51%reported increase in intent to reuse, per the agency partner

7×video engagement relative to benchmark

The challenge
Persuade residents to choose reusable bags, cups and utensils over disposable ones.
My ownership
Commissioned and directed the campaign, leading the agency, creative vendors and contractors.
Strategic approach
Framed reuse through fashion-inspired creative and invited residents to take part, through photo pop-ups, giveaways and a pledge, rather than only see ads.
Explore the project: Integrated brand campaign and audience engagement

Strategy and execution

Led integrated advertising, community activations, a pledge website and geotargeted digital promotion that drove attendance at neighborhood activations. User-generated-style video and culturally relevant creative reached Spanish- and Chinese-speaking, lower-income and older residents.

Results

  • +51% reported increase in intent to reuse
  • Video engagement at 7× benchmark, driven by the user-generated-style creative
  • Thousands of pledges and 4,000+ participant photos shared on social media, carrying the campaign into residents’ own channels
  • 2022 ADDY Award, Public Service Campaign

Campaign-level results reported by the agency partner. The 51% measures intent to reuse, not verified behavior.

City & County of San Francisco · SF Climate Action

Brand architecture and governance

+51%year-over-year website visits during the fourth annual Climate Action Month

The challenge
Bring the city’s environmental services and climate goals together under one coherent brand.
My ownership
Developed and governed the San Francisco Climate Action brand system, establishing shared identity, messaging and editorial standards across public-facing programs, campaigns and digital experiences.
Strategic approach
Extended the brand into Climate Action Month, a recurring citywide initiative, so residents met one consistent identity across programs, campaigns and events.
Explore the project: Brand architecture and governance

Brand system and governance

Expanded the city’s “0/80/100/Roots” climate identity into a brand system for city programs and public-facing communications, with naming, content strategy and governance for new products and citywide initiatives, coordinated across teams.

Extending the brand

Created Climate Action Month, an annual citywide initiative that brought the brand to residents through an integrated partner strategy. Its event-discovery portal is covered under Product Marketing.

Results

Website visits rose 51% year over year during the fourth annual Climate Action Month, reflecting the broader marketing and digital program rather than the brand system alone.

Broader context

  • San Francisco was named to the CDP (formerly Carbon Disclosure Project) Cities A List for the fourth consecutive year in 2022, recognizing global leadership in environmental transparency and climate action
  • The city reduced its carbon emissions while its population and economy grew, supported by the San Francisco Climate Action Plan and community engagement

These citywide achievements show the public mission the communications supported; they aren’t outcomes of the brand system.
